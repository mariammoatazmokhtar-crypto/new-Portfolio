About me

One person, start to finish

I am a 2D game designer and Unity developer specializing in puzzle and narrative-driven games. I help educators and edtech founders turn boring content into fun, playable experiences. I handle both the creative design and the C# implementation, so clients get one person who can deliver a polished, meaningful game from start to finish.

Creative design

Core loops, puzzles, story and level flow shaped around what your learners need to take away.

C# implementation

Gameplay systems, UI and dialogue built in Unity, so the design you approve is the game you get.

Polished delivery

A single point of contact from first idea to the final playable build.
